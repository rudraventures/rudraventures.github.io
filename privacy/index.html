Privacy

This page explains what this website collects from you, and what it does not.

What this website collects

This website has no form that sends information to a server or stores it, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. It does not require any personal information from you. Its fonts and images are served from this website itself, so opening a page does not send any request to another service.

Your language choice

When you choose English or Hindi, that choice is saved only in your own browser, so the page opens in the same language next time. It is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

Server records

Like most websites, the hosting service may keep standard technical records of visits, such as IP address, date and time, for security and operation. We do not use these records to identify visitors.

When you contact us

The unit pages carry a WhatsApp link. Opening it takes you to WhatsApp, which is run by another company under its own terms; nothing is sent until you send a message yourself. The enquiry boxes on the Rudra Realty Group and Rudra Wellness Group pages only prepare a message on your own device; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else until you send it yourself through WhatsApp or email. If you call or write to us, the information you share is used only to respond to you. It is not sold or given to any third party, and it is not entered into any external service.

Documents given for our services

Documents taken by Rudra Realty Group or Rudra Wellness Group for providing a service are covered by a separate notice, given to you at the time they are collected.

Your rights

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives individuals rights over their personal data, and its provisions are being brought into force in phases. We already honour these requests: you may ask what information we hold about you; ask for it to be corrected, completed, updated or erased; nominate a person to act for you; and raise a complaint with us. Write to info@rudraventures.in. Once the relevant provisions are in force, if you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

यह website क्या एकत्र करती है

इस website पर ऐसा कोई form नहीं है जो जानकारी किसी server पर भेजे अथवा संग्रहीत करे; कोई cookie नहीं रखी जाती, और कोई analytics अथवा विज्ञापन-उपकरण प्रयुक्त नहीं होता। आपसे कोई व्यक्तिगत जानकारी अनिवार्य रूप से नहीं माँगी जाती। इसके font एवं चित्र इसी website से दिए जाते हैं, अतः पृष्ठ खोलने पर किसी अन्य सेवा को कोई अनुरोध नहीं जाता।

जब आप English अथवा हिंदी चुनते हैं, तो वह चयन केवल आपके अपने browser में सहेजा जाता है, जिससे अगली बार पृष्ठ उसी भाषा में खुले। वह हमें नहीं भेजा जाता। Browser का site data मिटाकर आप उसे हटा सकते हैं।

Server के तकनीकी अभिलेख

अधिकांश websites की भाँति, hosting सेवा सुरक्षा एवं संचालन हेतु visits के सामान्य तकनीकी अभिलेख रख सकती है, जैसे IP address, दिनांक एवं समय। इन अभिलेखों से हम किसी आगंतुक की पहचान नहीं करते।
